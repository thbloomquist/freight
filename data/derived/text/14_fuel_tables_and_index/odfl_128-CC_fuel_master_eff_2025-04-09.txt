ITEM 1 — FUEL RELATED INCREASE — APPLICATION OF PERCENTAGE INCREASE (See A, B, C, and D)
1. All rates, arbitraries and charges named in tariffs or contract schedules and supplements thereto, made subject to this
   Master Tariff, are hereby or will on their effective dates be increased as provided below, for the period this tariff is in
   effect. Also applies on shipments from or to Canada.

2. In applying the provisions herein, first determine the applicable rate or charge including all applicable increases or
   discounts, if any. The rate or charge so determined will be further subject to the percentage increases provided
   herein.

3. Except as otherwise specifically provided, a fuel surcharge, as provided in Item 2, will be determined by the U.S.
   National Average Fuel Index for diesel fuel as published by the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA). The
   current Diesel Fuel Index can be obtained every Tuesday, (Wednesday if Monday is a federal holiday), by calling
   1.202.586.6966 or by visiting https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/. The EIA average will remain effective on
   Mondays (though published on Tuesdays).

    Adjustments will be made every Wednesday based on the EIA announcement effective Monday of the previous week.

NOTE A—Except as otherwise provided, fractions of less than one-half cent will be dropped; fractions of one-half cent or
      greater will be increased to the next whole cent.

NOTE B—Where a through rate is made by combining two or more factors, each is increased separately under the
      provisions of this item and the applicable through rate is the total of the combined factors.

NOTE C—Except as otherwise provided, each percentage calculation shall be rounded off to the nearest whole number
      and the percentage calculation will be subject to a minimum increase of $1.50.

NOTE D—For each 5 cents increase in the Fuel Index above 510 cents, increase the Fuel Surcharge by 0.5%.
