Item 1                                          FUEL RELATED INCREASE

                                      APPLICATION OF PERCENTAGE INCREASE
                                               (See A, B, C, D and F)


    All rates, arbitraries and charges named in tariffs or contract schedules and supplements thereto, made subject to
this Master Tariff, are hereby or will on their effective dates be increased as provided below, for the period this tariff is
in effect. Also applies on shipments from or to Canada.

   In applying the provisions herein, first determine the applicable rate or charge including all applicable increases or
discounts, if any. The rate or charge so determined will be further subject to the percentage increases provided herein.

   Except as otherwise specifically provided, a fuel surcharge, as provided in Item 2, will be determined by the U.S.
National Average Fuel Index for diesel fuel as published by the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA). The
current Diesel Fuel Index will be obtained each Monday by calling 1-202-586-6966 and the fuel surcharge will be
adjusted up or down if necessary each Wednesday based on the above referenced Index.

A – Except as otherwise provided, fractions of less than one-half cent will be dropped; fractions of one-half cent or
       greater will be increased to the next whole cent.

B – Where a through rate is made by combining two or more factors, each is increased separately under the
      provisions of this item and the applicable through rate is the total of the combined factors.

C – Except as otherwise provided, each percentage calculation shall be rounded off to the nearest whole number and
       the percentage calculation will be subject to a minimum increase of $1.50.

D – For each 5 cents increase in the Fuel Index above 510 cents, increase the Fuel surcharge by 0.5%.

F – E.I.A. is implementing new methodology to estimate weekly on–highway diesel fuel prices beginning
        June 13, 2022. Between June 9 and June 13, to gradually phase in prices from the new sample, the E.I.A.
        will revise prices that were published in some of the previous weeks.
        ODFL will not be amending fuel scales to reflect these updates.
        Previous announcement(s) by the E.I.A. shall apply.
