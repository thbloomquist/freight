                                                                           LESS THAN TRUCKLOAD FSC UNDER DOE 190-N - APPLICABLE THRU JANUARY 31, 2026
The LTL FSC shall be calculated each week using the DOE U.S. Average on Highway Diesel Price on Tuesday (unless Monday is a federal holiday, in which case the day will be Wednesday). This diesel price will then
be used to determine the respective FSC percentage based upon the FSC matrix (DOE 190-N Item 200). The applicable percentage in the matrix shall be the FSC starting on Wednesday immediately after the
Monday and running through the following Tuesday.

Example: If using the DOE 190-N, and the diesel price reported by DOE on Monday January 6 (first Monday in January) was $3.561 dollars per gallon, the FSC for LTL shipments offered for transportation under DOE
rate tenders during the period of Wednesday January 8 through Tuesday January 14 would be 14.0 %.

                                                                                 TRUCKLOAD FSC UNDER DOE 190-N APPLICABLE THRU JANUARY 31, 2026
The TL FSC shall be calculated each week using the DOE U.S. Average on Highway Diesel Price on Tuesday (unless Monday is a federal holiday, in which case the day will be Wednesday). This diesel price will then be
used to determine the respective FSC per mile rate increase based upon the FSC matrix (DOE 190-N Item 200). The applicable per mile rate in the matrix shall be the FSC starting on Wednesday immediately after
the Monday and running through the following Tuesday.

Example: If using the DOE 190-N, and the diesel price reported by DOE on Monday January 6 (first Monday in January) was $3.561 dollars per gallon, the FSC per mile rate for TL shipments offered for transportation
under DOE rate tenders during the period of Wednesday January 8 through Tuesday January 14 would be $0.27 per mile.

                                                                                 HOUSEHOLD GOODS FUEL SURCHARGE APPLICABLE UNTIL SUPERCEDED
HHG Fuel Surcharge (FSC) is determined on the 1st Monday of the month and goes into effect on the 15th of that month and runs through the 14th of the following month. Transporation and Drayage FSC are
calculated based upon the date of the activity.

Example: If the diesel rate on Monday January 6 (first Monday in January) was 3.561 dollars per gallon, the FSC of 14.0 % would be effective January 15 through February 14. If the household goods were picked up
on January 20, then this FSC rate would be in effect. However, If the household goods were put into storage (SIT) and then delivered (drayage) on May 16, the FSC for drayage would be based upon the diesel prices
on May 5 (first Monday in May). This FSC would be in effect for the drayage portion of the move.
