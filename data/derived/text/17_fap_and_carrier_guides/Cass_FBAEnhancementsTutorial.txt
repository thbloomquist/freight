Introduction
The interface for resolving rejected freight bill exceptions through the Freight Bill Authorization
(FBA) application on CassPort has been enhanced to make your portion of the resolution process
clearer, and allow confirmation so that you can review your resolution before submitting it. The
change is only to the interface – you have the same resolution options as before.
Enhancements have been made to FBA resolution for single invoices and mass modify resolution
for resolving multiple invoices at once.
The enhancements fall into several categories:
   Navigation – Enhancements to improve your ability to know where you are in the resolution
   process and confirm or change data before completing the process.
   Option Clarification – Enhancements to ensure all users understand the action being selected
   or reviewed.
   Standardized Reasons – Enhancements to reduce the amount of data entry required while
   providing useful information for carriers.
This document provides you with a quick tutorial showing you how these enhancements affect your
use of FBA to resolve rejected freight bill exceptions.

Conclusion
Confirmation dialogs, data entry configured for the resolution option you have selected, and a
separate stamp dialog will enable you to resolve bills in FBA more easily and accurately.
For your reference, here is a complete list of the enhancements by category:
   Navigation
       Only those fields related to the selected resolution option display for completion. So, the
       path for resolving the freight bill exception is always clearly delineated.
       The account number and name display at the top of the dialog to ensure you know the
       account under which the freight bill exception is being processed.
       The reprocess stamp remains hidden unless you choose to complete it. When you choose to
       complete it, the stamp displays in a separate dialog. So, you can review the freight bill
       information without scrolling while you are completing the stamp.
       For each resolution option, a confirmation dialog displays to enable you to review all entered
       or selected information before completing the resolution process.
       When using mass modify, all criteria selected for the freight bill exceptions included in the
       mass modification display at the top of all dialogs.
   Option Clarification
       The Approve option has been renamed Reprocess for clarification and consistency with
       suspense options. When you select Reprocess, the bill will be reprocessed at Cass with any
       options or instructions you have entered, such as processing the bill in a different shipper
       account. To approve a bill while reprocessing, simply fill out the approval stamp.
       The watermark on the Discard stamp has been changed from “Paper” to “Discard” so all
       resolution stamps match the resolution option selected.
   Standardized Reasons
       Easily selected standard reasons for an adjustment to the pay amount reduce the amount of
       data you must enter and provide a standardized reason for review. (single resolution only)
       Customizable reasons for a reject to the carrier reduce the amount of data you must enter
       and provide a standardized reject reason for review.
       The selected reason (adjustment or reject) displays with other information on the resolution
       stamp.
If you have additional questions, please contact your Cass Client Relations Representative.
