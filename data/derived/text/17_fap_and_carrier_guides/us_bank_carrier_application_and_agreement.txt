This U.S. Bank Freight Payment Service for Carriers Agreement (the “Agreement”) is between the entity signing the U.S.
Bank Freight Payment Service for Carriers Application as “Carrier” and U.S. Bank National Association (“U.S. Bank”). If

“Effective Date”).

1. DEFINITIONS: “Accelerated Payment” means U.S. Bank’s prompt initiation of a payment to Carrier on behalf of a
   Shipper on an invoice under a Shipper/Carrier Agreement after U.S. Bank’s receipt of the Shipper’s approval to pay
   such invoice. “Administrator” means a Carrier employee designated by Carrier to administer the Program.
   “Documentation” means the documentation provided to Carrier by U.S. Bank including, but not limited to, electronic
   and hard copy versions of manuals, instructions, policies, procedures, specifications, product standards, and guidelines
   for accessing and using the Platform, instructions for electronic debits and credits to account(s), reports, and
   information and display screens. “End User” means each individual issued End User Credentials by Administrator.
   “End User Credentials” means the electronic identity assigned to each End User permitting access to and use of the
   Platform, and functions as the End User’s electronic signature in the Platform. "Identification Information" means
   legal names, physical street addresses, taxpayer identification or business numbers, dates of births or other
   information or documentation. “Intellectual Property” means any patent rights, inventions, design rights, copyrights,
   database rights, trade secrets, trade names, trademarks, service marks, moral rights, know-how and any other similar
   rights or intangible assets recognized under any laws or international conventions, and in any country or jurisdiction in
   the world, as intellectual creations to which rights of ownership accrue, and all registrations, applications, disclosures,
   renewals, extensions, continuations or reissues of the foregoing now or hereafter in force. “Platform” means any online
   platform that is owned or licensed by U.S. Bank and through which parties may submit and process Transaction Data
   that will serve as the basis for U.S. Bank to make approved payments on behalf of Shippers. “Shipper” means any
   entity that has entered into a Shipper/Carrier Agreement with Carrier for transportation services. “Shipper/Carrier
   Agreement” means the agreement between Shipper and a Carrier regarding the provision of transportation services.
   “Transaction Data” means data and information regarding the provision of goods and/or services under a Shipper/
   Carrier Agreement.
2. SERVICES GENERALLY. U.S. Bank operates a Freight Payment Program (the “Program”) consisting of (i) providing
   enhanced services and functionality on the Platform and (ii) facilitating payments from Shippers to Carriers with
   Accelerated Payment. Carrier agrees to participate in the Program and U.S. Bank agrees to allow Carrier to participate
   in the Program, all in accordance with the terms contained herein.
3. PLATFORM ACCESS. Subject to the limitations set forth in Section 8, U.S. Bank shall provide Carrier access to the
   Platform to review and submit Transaction Data. Carrier agrees to U.S. Bank’s use of Transaction Data submitted by
   Carrier through the Platform to provide reconciliation services to the Shipper associated with such Transaction Data.
   Carrier’s access to the Platform is conditioned upon Carrier’s adherence to the Documentation, which may be
   amended and modified from time to time. At all times Carrier shall designate at least one Administrator to manage the
   Program. The Administrator shall be Carrier’s primary point of contact for U.S. Bank and shall be responsible for
   issuing End User Credentials. U.S. Bank shall be entitled to rely without further investigation on the directions provided
   by an Administrator or any party identified by an Administrator as having the authority to provide directions related to
   the Program.

        Bank and Carrier.
   6.3  Remedies. In an Event of Default (after the notice and cure period), in addition to any other remedies at
        equity or law, U.S. Bank may: (i) immediately cease making Accelerated Payments or immediately suspend

        costs incurred by U.S. Bank to exercise its rights or remedies under this section 6. For the purposes of any
        Event of Default under section 6.1.(ii), the due date on the applicable statement will constitute notice of Event
