TITLE: REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL – INBOUND FREIGHT MANAGEMENT SERVICES


1.     INTRODUCTION

     1.1.   Purpose

            The University of Pittsburgh – Of the Commonwealth System of Higher Education (referred to herein as the
            “University of Pittsburgh” or the “University”) is accepting proposals from qualified Contractors to provide
            inbound freight management services, as outlined in this Request for Proposal (RFP).

            The purpose of this RFP is to identify a Contractor capable of managing the University’s inbound freight
            program to ensure cost efficiency, timely delivery, streamlined operations, excellent customer service, and
            enhanced visibility into shipment tracking, invoicing, and freight activity.

            The selected Contractor will be expected to provide discounted pricing and value-added services for small
            parcel and less-than-truckload (LTL) shipments. Such as multi carrier support and execution, invoice and
            payment services (Freight audit and pay), supplier management and program support, customer support,
            reporting and analytics. In addition, the supplier should support the University’s goals to optimize freight
            spend, improve participating supplier compliance, convert unmanaged suppliers to the program, increase
            utilization, track and report performance metrics and more.

            The University is also interested in exploring volume-based discounts, customized reporting capabilities,
            and a freight management solution that integrates with existing systems and improves overall operational
            efficiency. This RFP outlines the specific requirements, terms, and conditions to guide the preparation of a
            detailed and professional proposal.

Current Invoicing Process

For participating suppliers, purchase orders generated in PantherExpress are exported daily to the freight
management provider, who then matches Purchase Order (PO) data to corresponding shipment records.
On a weekly basis, the provider compiles invoice data into Excel files separately for LTL shipments and FedEx
small parcel, that include detailed shipment, billing, and cost center information. These files are submitted
every week to the University's secure SFTP server for automated ingestion into the Prism interface.

Upon submission, Oracle generates either a “Successful” or “Failure” email notification. Failures typically
result from inactive or closed GL account codes, which cause the entire file to be rejected. When this occurs,
the provider contacts the purchase order owner to update the account information before resubmitting the
corrected file. Once successfully imported into Prism, the Payment Processing team performs an open
payable import process, receives the Excel and PDF invoice copies via a shared mailbox, and completes a
spot audit and invoice validation prior to payment.

Note* PantherExpress is the cloud-based purchasing tool and preferred procurement and payment
mechanism for most goods and services purchased at the University. PRISM is the University’s financial ERP
system, used for processing invoices and payments. It is built on Oracle’s E-Business Suite, which serves as
the underlying enterprise software platform. “GL” General Ledger.

The Contractor shall provide discounted inbound freight transportation rates and administrative services under a
managed inbound freight program. These two areas will be evaluated and priced separately to allow for a more

outlined in Section 1 of the RFP and shall include the following deliverables:

    1. Transportation Rates (Discounted Carrier Rates)

Contractor’s must propose freight rates for small parcel and LTL services. These rates can be provided under a third-
party reseller model and/or through access to existing aggregation/GPO agreements. The service fee should be
added to each line item (shipment) on the invoice, so the fee can be charged to the appropriate cost center at the
time of invoicing.

Contractor’s are required to submit levels of pricing using the documents provided in Attachment H_Inbound
Freight Management Services RFP – Pricing Proposal Workbook:

Standard Rate Table - UPS and FedEx (Small Parcel)

    •    Provide rates for small parcel shipments including and more, examples:
             o Ground
             o 2-Day
             o Overnight/Air Express
    •    Submit rates for each service level by weight tier or zone, if applicable.

LTL Pricing

    •    Submit rate tables by zone, weight, freight class, and delivery type
    •    Clearly identify base rates (e.g. CzarLite) and include breakdowns of:
             o Fuel surcharges
             o Accessorial fees

             o Liftgate or inside delivery charges

Net Pricing Sheet (Small Parcel)

    •    This should not include the service fee amount if you are bidding rates and services separately

    •    Vendors must price each shipment as if they had moved it under their program
