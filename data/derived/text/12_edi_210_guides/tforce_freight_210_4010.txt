                                     Business Policies



   Invoice are transmitted once per day.

   Customers can choose between receiving prepaid, collect or third party invoices.

   Can provide electronically the original, corrected or balance due invoices.

   When the customer requests sending electronic invoices written authorization must be
    sent to upgfedigroup@tforcefreight.com.

   Once set up electronic invoices, copies of the delivery receipts or bills of lading are no
    longer mailed to the customer.

   The payment terms for your paper invoices are applied to your EDI invoices.
