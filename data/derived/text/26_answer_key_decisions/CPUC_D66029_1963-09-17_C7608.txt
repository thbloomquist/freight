             On May 1, 1963, the Commission instituted its investiga-
tion into the operations, rates, charges and practices of Denio
Bros. Trucking Co.
             Public he3ring was held before Examiner Porter on July 11,

             The purpose of this investigation is to determine whether
respondent, in violation of Sections 3664 and 3667 of the Public
Utilities Code has charged, demanded or received a lesser compensa-
tion for the transportation of property than the applicable charges
prescribed in Minimum Rate Tariff No.2, and supp1c~ents thereto.
             The staff selected a review period of July 1, 1962 to
September 30, 1962.    During tI1is period the carrier had 377 rateable
shipments.    All were examined.   Eighteen were selected as representa-
tive and forwarded to the Rate Analysis Unit of the Commission and
undercharges were found in each instance.    The undercharges resulted
in general from (1) a failure to assess off rail charges when appli-
cable, (2) assessing a rail rate but not observing applicable mini-
mum weights, and (3) assessing a rate based on a judgment figure in
