                                    SERVICE SCHEDULE NO. 4 - TRANSPORTATION SERVICES
                  This Service Schedule No. 4 for Transportation Services ("Schedule") is attached to and made part of that certain Master
         Services Agreement dated November 6, 2006, (the "MSA") by and between UPS Supply Chain Solutions, Inc. ("SCS") and Actavis,
         Inc., ("Client") as of November 6, 2006 (the "Effective Date"). The terms and conditions of the MSA are incorporated herein by
         reference. Terms not defined in this Schedule shall have the meaning set forth in the MSA.
         1. Provision of Services. SCS will provide transportation services to support Client's logistics requirements related to
         transportation (the "Services"), in accordance with this Schedule and all bills of lading and other transportation documents issued by
         SCS and carriers, each of which is incorporated into this Schedule by reference. In the event of a conflict between any bill of lading
         or other transportation document and this Schedule, this Schedule will control. Client acknowledges that this Schedule does not
         provide for the handling or transportation of Hazardous, Dangerous or Regulated Goods. Unless otherwise specified in a Services
         Schedule, SCS shall not be required to arrange for transportation of Goods in a special environment, including but not limited to air-
         conditioned, cooled, chilled or frozen environments.
         2. Term and Termination. The Initial Term of this Schedule shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue up to and
         including November 5, 2009 ("Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated in accordance with the MSA. Thereafter, this Schedule will
         automatically renew for successive one-year renewal terms, unless either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal at
         least one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. In addition to the termination provisions in the
         MSA, this Schedule may be terminated by either Party: (a) without cause upon one hundred twenty (120) days prior written notice or
         (b) with cause upon the commission of a material breach of this Schedule which is not cured within sixty (60) days after the
         breaching Party receives notice.
         3. Termination Costs. SCS must make certain financial commitments in order to perform the Services, which SCS expects to
         recover during the term of this Services Appendix. As a consequence, if this Schedule is terminated (i) by SCS due to Client's
         breach, failure to make payments when due, insolvency, or bankruptcy, or (ii) by Client for its convenience, Client shall pay SCS the
         termination cost listed in Exhibit A, if any.
         4. Fees. The Fees applicable to the Services and the Operating Parameters (as defined below) on which SCS has calculated the
         Fees are specified on Exhibit A. SCS will invoice Client every two weeks for the Fees as incurred by SCS. Client will pay all Fees
         on an invoice within fourteen (14) days from date of the invoice. The Fees will be reviewed annually. SCS will use commercially
         reasonable efforts to minimize or eliminate annual price increases from its core carriers. However, when carrier price increases are
         incurred, SCS will pass such price increases on to Client, and Client will pay them, notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this
         Schedule. Any surcharges imposed or paid will be per section 3.0 of the MSA.
         5. Operating Parameters. SCS has calculated the Fees set forth on Exhibit A based on the Operating Parameters set forth in
         Exhibit B. The following provision supersedes any conflicting provision of the MSA with regard to this Schedule: In the event of a
         change in any Operating Parameter, SCS will propose an adjustment to the Fees to respond to the change by providing Client with
         fifteen (15) days prior written notice thereof, setting forth with reasonable specificity the basis for such adjustment. Client shall not
         unreasonably withhold its approval of such adjustment. If Client refuses to pay such adjustment, either Party may immediately
         escalate the matter to senior managers of each Party. Thereafter, if the Parties have still not reached an agreement, the Parties may
         proceed with resolution of the dispute or termination in accordance with the MSA.
        6. Liability for Loss or Damage. SCS shall use commercially reasonable efforts to require all carriers (including carriers that are
        affiliates of SCS) selected by SCS for the purposes of transporting Goods to maintain motor cargo insurance in the amount of
        $100,000 to cover lost or damaged Goods caused by carrier's negligence. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Client acknowledges and
        agrees that the carrier of the Goods shall have the risk of any loss of or damage to the Goods managed pursuant to this Schedule
        while the Goods are in such carrier's care, custody, or control on such terms and conditions as may be agreed to in writing between
        SCS and the carrier. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, SCS shall have no liability for any loss of or
        damage to any Goods transported by a carrier (including carriers that are affiliates of SCS) and, in each such event, any claim of loss
        of or damage to such Goods shall be governed by the applicable standard terms and conditions of such carrier. In no event shall
        SCS have any responsibility to pay any amounts which a carrier fails or refuses to pay with respect to any carrier claim.
        7. Carrier Claims. Upon Client's request and payment of the associated Fees specified on Exhibit A, SCS will file on Client's
        behalf any claim for any loss or damage to Goods, provided that Client gives SCS written notice of such claim within thirty (30)
        days after delivery of the Goods by SCS' s carrier or thirty (30) days after Client is given written notice by SCS that loss or damage
        to the Goods has occurred, whichever is earlier. If Client fails to provide SCS notice of a claim in accordance with this Section,
        Client's right to bring such claim is waived. SCS will act on behalf of Client in the filing and processing of such claims, and Client
        will reimburse SCS for all reasonable costs and expenses incurred by SCS in connection with the filing and prosecution of such
        claims.
        8. Disclaimer. SCS is not responsible for any consequences that result from any action of Client in following or declining to
        follow any advice of SCS, it being acknowledged and agreed by Client that all decision-making regarding Client, including without
        limitation whether or not to follow any advice given by SCS, is solely the responsibility of Client.

        In accordance with this Schedule, Client will pay to SCS the following Fees:
        1.      Maintenance Fee: 5 % on weekly invoiced transportation expense for TL and LTL.
        2.      Surcharge for invoices received from carriers not identified in the SCS Routing Guide.
        3.      Pricing does not include fuel surcharges. UPS Supply Chain Solutions sets fuel surcharges (if applicable) weekly based on

                percentage of linehaul.
        4.      Accessorial charges will be charged to the Client as incurred.

        6.      Fees for processing carrier claims $65 per claim or 3% of the claim amount, whichever is greater.
        7.      Customs clearance, duties, entry fees are not included in the pricing.
        8.      Termination fees $0.00.
