            Benefits of Using DCL Freight Account
                 • Substantial discounts off carrier book rates
                 • Customer service for all carrier calls with regard to correct delivery of the order
                 • Tracking and application of carrier delivery credits for late delivery
                 • Receipt of undeliverable packages for customer disposition
                 • Easy customer reconciliation as freight charges are linked to each order
                 • eFactory freight reports showing freight charges by order and by carrier service
                 • Monthly freight service billing as opposed to weekly billing by carrier

            Additional Charges
            Additional freight charges above the standard transportation charges include: fuel surcharges, address
            corrections, insurance, residential delivery, out-of-area delivery and dimensional weight. These charges
            will be added to the standard transportation charge. The corresponding service level discount will be
            applied to the list fuel service charge. No discount is applied for other additional charges.

            DCL Preferred Carriers
            DCL reserves the right to substitute carriers on shipments while maintaining equivalent service and
            price.
