                   4.1      Definitions.

                           4.1.1   "Throughput" shall mean the number of Products received and shipped divided by two (2), between
          successive Determination Dates, or if within the first year of the Effective Date, then such first year.

                             4.1.2     "Replacement Cost" shall mean Client's actual cost to replace a Lost Product, i.e., the reasonable
          and customary cost incurred by Client to purchase and transport the Product from its supplier to the Facility; provided,
          however, that the Replacement Cost shall not include margin, markup or storage fees. Within thirty (30) days of the Effective
          Date and at least quarterly thereafter, Client shall provide to SCS appropriate documentation verifying the Replacement Cost of
          each Product.

                            4.1.3     "Average Replacement Cost" shall mean the weighted average of the Replacement Costs of all Lost
          Products.

                             4.1.4     "Determination Date" shall mean the yearly anniversary of the Effective Date upon which SCS's
          liability to Client, if any, for Lost Products and Damaged Products is determined; provided however, that if this Schedule
          expires or is terminated prior to such Determination Date, the revised Determination Date shall be the date of termination or
          expiration of this Schedule.

                             4.1.5  "Shrinkage Allowance" shall mean 0.2% of Throughput as of the Determination Date. The Parties
          have agreed to a Shrinkage Allowance because both Parties recognize that it is inevitable that a certain amount of Products will
          be lost or damaged in the course of storage and handling whether the Products are stored by SCS or by the Client at its own
          locations using its own employees.

                            4.1.6    "Lost Products" shall mean those Products, except for controlled substances, that should be
          warehoused on the Determination Date according to the Book Inventory Report, but that have been lost due to SCS's
          negligence or are otherwise unable to be located. Lost Products shall not include Damaged Products, concealed shortages, or
          Products shipped in error to Client's location or to a location designated by Client.

                           4.1.7   "Damaged Products" shall mean those Products warehoused on the Determination Date that are
          destroyed or damaged due to SCS's negligence such that they cannot be sold.

                             4.1.8    "Book Inventory Report" shall mean the report of those Products that should be warehoused as of
          the Determination Date based on (i) Beginning Inventory, i.e. the Products that were warehoused based on the Book Inventory
          Report as of the previous Determination Date or, if during the first year, then the Effective Date, plus (ii) Products received as
          evidenced by an authorized signature on the transportation carrier's waybill, since previous Determination Date or, if during
          the first year, then the Effective Date, less (iii) Products shipped since the previous Determination Date or, if during the first
          year, then the Effective Date. The Book Inventory Report will be generated by SCS using its warehouse inventory
          management systems, regardless of whether Client's inventory system is also used in connection with the Services.

                          4.1.9   "Repair Cost" shall mean the lesser of the cost to repair the Damaged Product or the Replacement
          Cost of the Damaged Product.

                            4.1.10 "Net Value of Inventory Adjustments" shall mean as of a Determination Date the sum of all the
          inventory adjustments for unaccounted for Products, based on the number of units, that are made by SCS in all Facilities during
          its cycle count process between successive Determination Dates or, if during the first year of the Effective Date, then during
          such first year. SCS will maintain a cumulative record of inventory adjustments completed.

                            4.1.11   "Excess Loss" shall mean the number of Lost Products multiplied by the Average Replacement
          Cost.

                            4.1.12 "Physical Inventory" shall mean a physical inventory of the Products. Any Physical Inventory will
          be conducted in a manner mutually acceptable to the Parties. If Client requests SCS to conduct such a Physical Inventory, SCS
          will perform the Physical Inventory at Client's sole expense.
