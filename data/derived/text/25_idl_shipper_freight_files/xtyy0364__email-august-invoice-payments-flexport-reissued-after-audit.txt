 For everyone's benefit let me just summarize a few points of background and ongoing efforts underway on the freight
 process.



 In the beginning part of the year we were having significant vendor compliance issues with Flexport. They were
 declaring incorrect entities on import documentation and rates were questionable. This led to Logistics putting a full
 stop to all invoices (both being processed and paid) until a full 100% audit was completed. The COO of Flexport and
 his senior staff was also brought in for a relationship discussion that resulted in us being issued an entirely new
 program management team.

 over to us last month. This is why there has been a very large number of invoices landing on approvers for the past
 6-8 weeks as that backlog is still unwinding its way through the Stampli approval process.



 From a process transformation perspective Nelson's team is in process of rolling out a freight bill audit and payment
 company (CTSI) to outsource the rate validation. We're testing this flow with 1 US carrier now and we're seeing
 about a 70% automated approval rate / 30% requires manual review sign off. There's a decision meeting next week to
 decide if we're at a stable enough status to incorporate Flexport onto it.



 In terms of spend review, we have a weekly checkpoint with Flexport over their aging to ensure they're current and
 we're not at risk of any service interruptions. As of this week we were approximately 85% current / 15% past due
 with them, and they have visibility to the path to get them fully current.
