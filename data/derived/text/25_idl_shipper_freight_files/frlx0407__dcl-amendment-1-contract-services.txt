                                           AMENDMENT NO. 1
                                                 TO
                                     CONTRACT SERVICES AGREEMENT

         This Amendment No. 1 to Contract Services Agreement is made as of this th day of April,
         2016, by and between DisCopyLabs, Inc. ("DCL") and PAX Labs, Inc. (formerly known as
         "Ploom, Inc.") ("Customer," and together with DCL, collectively referred to herein as the
         "Parties").

         WHEREAS, DCL and Customer are parties to that certain Contract Services Agreement, entered
         into as of October 17, 2014 (the "Agreement"); and

         WHEREAS, the Parties wish to amend the Agreement.

         NOW THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which
         are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

          1.    Interpretation/Definitions.

                 1.1     The terms hereof shall constitute an amendment to the Agreement. Except as
         provided herein, the terms and conditions of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.
         In the event of conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, the terms and conditions of
         this Amendment shall control.

                1.2     Capitalized terms used herein but not otherwise defined herein shall have the
          meanings ascribed thereto in the Agreement.

         2.      New Schedule. New Schedule A-1, attached hereto, with respect to applicable pricing for
         services at DCL's Kentucky facility, is hereby added to the Agreement. References to "Schedule
         A" in the Agreement shall be deemed to also refer to Schedule A-1 to the extent applicable.

         3.   Term and Termination. The section of the Agreement captioned "TERM AND
         TERMINATION" is hereby amended as follows:

                3.1     The first sentence thereof is hereby amended to read as follows:

                "This Agreement shall remain in effect until October 17, 2019, and shall be renewed
                automatically for further periods of one (1) year each unless notice of cancellation in
                writing is sent by either party to the other no later than one hundred eighty (180) days
                before the expiration of the then-current term."

                3.2    The reference to "ninety (90) days" in subpart (a) of the second sentence thereof is
                hereby changed to "one hundred eighty (180) days."

         4.   Compensation. The fifth sentence of the section of the Agreement captioned
         "COMPENSATION," which states that "Warehouse storage charges for goods remaining longer
