     ADDENDUM TO PROOF OF CLAIM OF TOTAL QUALITY LOGISTICS, LLC

        The claimant, Total Quality Logistics, LLC (“TQL”), holds a claim against the Debtor in
the total amount of $275,915.66, as summarized on the attached Exhibit A.1 All such amounts are
claimed as defenses to equal amounts alleged to be due to the Debtor by TQL and are subject to
TQL’s rights of recoupment and/or setoff, among other rights (all of which TQL expressly
reserves) as may arise under applicable law.

        Since shortly after the commencement of the Debtor’s chapter 11 case, TQL and the Debtor
have engaged in extensive discussions regarding potential resolution of the amounts alleged to be
due to the Debtor by TQL as well as the amounts due by the Debtor to TQL, including those
summarized on the attached Exhibit A.2 In the ordinary course of business between the parties, as
well as part of such discussions regarding potential resolution, TQL has provided further detailed
information and documentation to the Debtor with respect to the basis, and in support of, the
amounts set forth on the attached Exhibit A. Copies of the documents and further detailed
information supporting and providing further basis for TQL’s claim set forth in this proof of claim
(collectively, the “Documents”) are voluminous, and as such, have not been attached hereto, but
can be submitted as may be required in the future. TQL believes that the Debtor is in possession
of such Documents and TQL will also make such Documents available to the Debtor upon written
request sent to Total Quality Logistics, LLC, Attn: Joseph B. Wells, Corporate Counsel, 4289 Ivy
Pointe Blvd., Cincinnati, Ohio 45245 or jwells@tql.com. If copies of the Documents are required
to be filed with the Court or requested by any other party in interest, TQL reserves all rights,
including, without limitation, the right to request to file the Documents under seal and/or to seek
to protect such information as confidential pursuant to Section 107 of title 11 of the United States
Code (the “Bankruptcy Code”) or otherwise applicable law, to the extent such Documents may
contain or comprise confidential and/or proprietary information.

        TQL reserves the right to amend or supplement this proof of claim from time to time and
at any time. Without limiting the foregoing, TQL reserves the right to amend or supplement this
proof of claim to assert any additional invoice disputes, overcharges, credits, discounts, cargo
claims and/or any other amounts due to TQL by the Debtor within no less than the time period(s)
provided by any applicable law, including, without limitation, 49 U.S.C. § 14706(e)(1), as well as
any other federal transportation laws and regulations, as may be applicable.
