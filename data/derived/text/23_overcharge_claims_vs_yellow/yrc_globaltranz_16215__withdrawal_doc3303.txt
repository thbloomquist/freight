                                                                      DEFINITIONS
Debtor
The person, corporation, or other entity that has filed a bankruptcy case is called the debtor.

Creditor
A creditor is any person, corporation, or other entity to which the debtor owed a debt.

Proof of Claim
A form filed with the clerk of the bankruptcy court where the bankruptcy case was filed, to tell the bankruptcy court how much the debtor owed a
creditor (the amount of the creditor's claim).
