# C-09 open questions — the ten that decide it

The ten customer-interview questions that decide C-09, as given in chat on 2026-10-09 (session `04a9c862`). The full list of about 40 is in `C09-open-questions-evidence.md` §0. A fuller copy of these ten, with the founders' original wording for F1-F4, the assumption-register IDs and the rules for asking, is in `C09-business-proposal.md` §8.1.

F1-F4 are the founders' own four questions.

| # | Question | What it decides |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **F1:** what share of freight dollars is billed wrong? Ask about the last wrong bill, then the last 12 months in dollars against spend | **Test 1:** Kill, Cover or Pass. Only the look-back proves it |
| 2 | How much do you spend a year on truckload, LTL and parcel, and how much goes through brokers? | Which row of the Kill/Cover/Pass table applies, and which claim deadline |
| 3 | **F2:** who looked into the last wrong bill, how long did it take, and with what tool? | Whether staff work the flags (the middle band). Whether an incumbent already does the job (the show-stopper) |
| 4 | **F4:** how many disputes did you file with carriers in 12 months, and how many were credited? Has an outside firm audited you? | How much carriers credit; whether customers renew |
| 5 | **F3:** how much do you think you're overcharged? Follow with "What makes you say that?" | Logged as a belief only: whether the "scoop off the top" pitch lands |
| 6 | Will you upload last month's invoices and your rate agreements under an NDA? | **Test 2:** kill if 2 of the first 3 shippers say no |
| 7 | How many hours did your team actually spend on freight bills last month? | **Test 3:** whether the flag queue fits their hours |
| 8 | Where do your rate agreements live, for how many carriers, and how often do they change? | Test 3: how long setup takes |
| 9 | What do you pay today to check bills? Who signs off on software, and up to what amount? | Whether a subscription can be bought at all |
| 10 | Do your contracts set a deadline for disputes? How soon after paying do you notice a wrong bill? | How much spend is still recoverable, which is what Test 1 divides by |
