## Reader's judgement on relevance to a two-sided freight sub-market choice
*Judgement:* this is the canonical origin of the three axes that let a freight sub-market be written as a cell rather than a label: shipper groups (customer groups), shipping jobs (customer functions), and the way the job is currently done (technologies, read broadly as "how", per R06's forum note). That includes asset carriers, brokers, 3PLs, spreadsheets and phone, or software. The index term "Alternative Technologies" matters for a software entrant, because software is an alternative "technology" serving an existing customer function. The book itself could not be read; any detail beyond R06 would need a library copy.
