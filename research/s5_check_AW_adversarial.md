# S5 check AW, adversarial review

- **What this is:** an evidence-backed adversarial check of check AW (the field map of shipment-fact documents) and of the parts of `data/doc-explanation.md` that rest on it. Each claim was re-opened at the cited file and page and tested to see whether it breaks. No objection was invented; a claim that survives is reported as a result too.
- **Files checked:** `research/s5_check_AW_c09_field_map_shipment_facts.md` (§0 summary, §3 "who wins" table, §4 limits, §8 ledger of 34 rows); `data/doc-explanation.md` §1.1 (Rate quote and Rate confirmation *Held* lines), §1.2 (BOL, delivery receipt, W&I entries), §4.3, §4.6 and §4.8 example D. Neither file was edited.
- **Date:** 2026-10-10.
- **Status:** input to S5. **It decides nothing.**
- **What was opened:** every page cited in the ledger, from the PDFs in `data/sources/`, rendered with `pdftoppm` (60-400 dpi) and viewed, or read from `data/derived/text/`. Set 04 was re-OCRed page by page (853 pages, tesseract with the project's `data/_tools/tessdata`). Also opened: the set 13 tariff texts for every item AW quotes, plus SEFL 360-5, 647 and 830, Estes 567, ABF's residential item and XPO's 2018 Item 239; the set 15 standards; all eight 214s and four 204s in set 19; the catalogue rows of every file tagged `rate_confirmation`, `bol`, `delivery_receipt` or `B5`; 22-007 (TQL invoices); 21-001 (ODFL); 24-007 (W&I vendor). All arithmetic was redone in `python3 -I`. Renders and OCR are in the session scratchpad, outside the project.
- **No web fetches.** No identity was sent anywhere. No individual's name, signature or phone number is transcribed here, and no street address is given.
- **Not checked, and why:** AW §2 field rows that are not in the ledger were sampled, not all re-read (e.g. Werner $2,800 on 22-013, KCH 8615027 dates on 10-043, 06-016 p. 11, 21-002 p. 6, 05-015 p. 7, 09-048, 12-049, 19-036). The doc's §1.2 BOL sentences that rest on checks AT and AV (bill 807148067's echo of the BOL, the 2,430 lb derivation, the redelivery bill) are AV's to review. §4.6's four-state table and the First Brands sentence rest on AV and AX. R+L's residential definition was not re-read.

**Labels:** **KEEP-verified** = the sentence is true as written, about its own population, on the file cited. **KEEP-assumed** = it stands but is a reading, not a fact on the page. **NARROW** = a smaller or corrected reading survives (given). **DISCARD** = the source contradicts it.

## §3 Counter-examples searched for, and what was found

1. **Certificate pages in set 04, page-level count.** All 853 pages of 04-006 to 04-014 were rendered at 150 dpi and OCRed one page at a time. Three passes:
   - (a) keyword classes per page, allowing for garbled OCR: weight-certificate variants ("W…T CERTIFI…"), "Scale Total", "Weighed Date", "Weighing performed", "mentioned shipment"; and "Inspection Certificate", "Shipment Density", "Adequate Packaging", "double stack". Result: 43 and 6 candidates.
   - (b) the certificate header ("P.O. Box 1216", "23218"). It found 11 more pages, all invoice summaries or BOLs.
   - (c) a layout sweep: every page reduced to 32×40 pixels and correlated with the mean of the confirmed certificates. The confirmed pages have a median of 0.946; the best non-candidate scores 0.59. The 12 highest non-candidates were viewed, and none is a certificate.

   All 49 candidates were viewed on contact sheets: **43 weight certificates, 6 inspection certificates**. The PROs readable at 400 dpi run in ascending order with no repeat, so the pages are not duplicates. One page (04-013 p. 68) is a later, larger-type layout. The catalogue tags `wi_certificate` on 04-008 only.
2. **Certificate arithmetic elsewhere.** The three other typed UPS inspection certificates, the TForce cube certificate, and the unit sums on five UPS weight certificates (655; 640+320+320+320 = 1,600; 3,460; 2,340; 1,295) all reproduce. 406858966 is the only failure found.
3. **A DR printed before the reweigh:** found (04-009 p. 16), which makes B7 and D14.
4. **A DR used to record a service request:** found (Estes 567 ¶2), which makes D16.
5. **A 214 reason code other than NS or NA:** found (BK, 19-017), which makes B5.
6. **A shipper-facing TL price:** found as billed amounts (22-007 read; 22-001, 22-006, 22-008, 22-015, 22-016 from catalogue rows). No agreed-price document other than the 204 was found. This makes B4 and D2.
7. **A rate confirmation issued to a shipper:** none. The roles were read directly in 7 of the 18 files.
8. **A quote applied without being on the BOL:** SEFL 360-5 ¶6 provides for it, which makes B8 and D1.
9. **Other bills to the Ocean City consignee.** Set 02 holds ten bill pages to Ocean City, MD, for six consignees: Island Wear (pp. 31/44, 50, 53), NY Piercing (p. 62), Sunsations (p. 64), Cool Topics (p. 75, charged limited access $50, not residential) and Ocean Waves (pp. 60, 93, 94). p. 58 goes to Ocean City, NJ (Shore Amusement), with "DEL TO A PRIVATE RESIDENCE". Only pp. 60, 93 and 94 carry the Ocean Waves street address. The worksheet (pp. 99-224) names "Ocean Waves" consignees on about 42 rows, without addresses. **The doc's claim survives.**
10. **Satellite imagery on the carrier's side in 23-003:** not found. Yellow cited a listing (B3).
11. **An ODFL delivery receipt in set 21**, which the doc's DR *Held* line implies: found (21-001 pp. 19, 20, 26). **Survives.**
12. **Tariff text on what rebuts a carrier reweigh:** none in the items read (B2, D9).
13. **Older tariff editions:** XPO's 2018 Item 239 had a different fee and trigger (ledger #16), which bears out AW's *assumed* tag.
14. **ABF quote notes split across lines:** 5 more found (ledger #27).
15. **Other carriers' certificates elsewhere in `data/`:** the text layers mention certificates in XPO bills ("RE-WEIGHED PER WEIGHT CERTIFICATE") and in the ODFL and TForce document-API guides, which list certificate document types. No further certificate images were found.
16. **The BOL's internal arithmetic:** the Holland BOL's lines (131.49 kg) do not sum to its total (149.59 kg). This supports the doc's own rule "sum the lines against the grand total".
