# S5 check AP, adversarial review (contacts)

- **What this is:** the adversarial check of the C-09 contacts list. The founders asked for contacts "as many as possible, as reliable as possible" and for "adversarial agents for the web resources so we prune any coincidences and can settle the facts ourselves or throw away faulty web data". Each contact was tested on seven points:
  1. Is the person real and in the role now?
  2. Does their own work match the questions they are tagged with?
  3. Is the route published where the list says?
  4. Were the privacy rules kept?
  5. Is any conflict of interest missing?
  6. Is the contact a duplicate?
  7. Is the grade right?
- **Files checked:**
  - the private contacts list (61 named people, 21 organisational routes; kept outside this folder);
  - `research/s5_check_AP_c09_contacts.md` (method and counts);
  - the question IDs in `C09-open-questions-evidence.md` §2.
- **Date:** 2026-10-07.
- **Status:** input to S5. **It decides nothing.** Names are kept outside the project: the same verdicts, with names and the leads found, are in the private review file next to the contacts list.
- **Searches used:** 6 of 10 (currency checks only), plus about 95 direct fetches of the cited pages, all on 2026-10-07.
- **No identity was sent. Nobody was contacted.** No people-search or data-broker site was opened, and no LinkedIn page was opened.
- **Scope covered:** all 46 A and B contacts got all seven tests. All 15 C contacts were checked as well (the brief asked for at least 5). All 21 organisational routes were checked for test 3.
- **How tags were judged:** a tag stays when the cited work, case or role puts the answer in the person's hands. A tag resting only on a general field ("transportation law", "freight", a committee's scope) was removed. A contact with no tag left is marked **referral only**: worth asking "who would know?", not the question itself.
- **How doubts were handled:** a doubt with no evidence behind it is logged as "concern, unproven" and leaves the contact as it was.
