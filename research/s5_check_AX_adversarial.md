# S5 check AX, adversarial review

- **What this is:** an evidence-backed adversarial check of check AX (`research/s5_check_AX_c09_field_map_price_terms.md`, written 2026-10-10), the field map of what a shipment *should* cost and what a dispute must contain. It also checks the parts of `data/doc-explanation.md` that rest on AX. Each claim was tested to see whether it breaks. No objection was invented, and a claim that survives is reported as a result too.
- **Files checked:** AX's claim ledger (§9, rows 1-31), the accessorial price matrix (§2.2), the precedence list (§3), the three worked examples (§4), the trigger wording (§5), the clocks table (§6), and AX §1-§2 and §7-§8 where a ledger row rests on them. In `data/doc-explanation.md`: §1.1 (pricing agreement, rules tariff with the residence bullet, fuel surcharge table, rate confirmation format, plus one rate-quote sentence that rests on AX §3.7), §1.4 (overcharge claim, the law), §2's "6 carriers", §3's base-tariff and tariff-edition rows, §4.4, §4.5, §4.7, and §4.8 examples A and B. Neither file was edited.
- **Date:** 2026-10-10.
- **Status:** input to S5. **It decides nothing.**
- **What was opened:**
  - **Text layers** in `data/derived/text/`: sets 01, 02, 08, 10, 11, 12, 13 (the tariffs of SEFL, ABF in all three issues, ODFL, Estes, XPO, R+L, Saia, FedEx Freight, TForce, AAA Cooper, Central, Dayton, Werner and USA Truck), 14, 16, 19, 20, 22 and 23.
  - **Page images**, rendered with `pdftoppm` into the session scratchpad and viewed:
    - 02-002 pp. 19, 21 and 22, with 300-dpi crops of the p. 22 liftgate and redelivery rows;
    - 23-006 pp. 4-5;
    - 23-012 pp. 88-89.
  - **OCR:** 23-012 pp. 74-96 re-OCR'd with the project's own tessdata (`data/_tools/tessdata`).
  - **EIA series:** 14-074 converted to CSV with LibreOffice headless.
  - **Estes table:** 14-076's fuel table parsed from the page script.
  - **Arithmetic and dates** re-done in `python3 -I`, half-up to the cent.
- **Web:** one fetch. 49 CFR Part 378 came from the eCFR versioner API, version 2026-09-01 (`https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-09-01/title-49.xml?part=378`). The request used a generic browser user-agent with compression and sent no identity. No other site was contacted.
- **Not checked, and why:**
  - **Home Depot's Schedule A-1 row values.** AX already tags the column split *assumed*.
  - **GM (11-038) and Schneider (11-040).** Neither has a usable text layer.
  - **The doc's SMC3, FEMA and NMFC cost figures** in §3. They rest on check AR, whose adversarial review agreed (`s5_check_AR_adversarial.md` AR3). They were not re-fetched.
  - **Two §1.4 sentences that rest on checks AO and AT, not AX.** These are "brokers give 5-30 days" and "Central Transport use[s] its own web form". No Central Transport claim form is in `data/`.
  - **AX §7's field table.** Only ABF's, YRC's and XPO's cells were checked cell by cell.
  - **TForce Item 830-A's residence wording.**
  - **The NMFC claim items (e.g. 300205).** NMF 100 is not held.
- **Labels used here:**
  - **KEEP-verified:** the sentence is true as written, about its own population.
  - **KEEP-assumed:** the sentence rests on an inference the files cannot prove.
  - **NARROW:** a smaller reading survives, and it is given.
  - **DISCARD:** the cited source does not say it.

**Body statements outside the ledger that need the same treatment:**
- **B1. AX §3.5 drops part of SEFL 201 Note B.** AX quotes "the pricing published specifically for the logistics company ... will apply", but the elided words reverse part of the meaning. The full text (13-065 line 384) reads: "the pricing published specifically for the logistics company, consultant, or other entity, **or for the party they represent** will apply". ODFL 764 and AAA Cooper 760 Note B say the same and add "unless specific instructions to the contrary are received from the beneficial owner". **NARROW:** a 3PL payor can carry either its own pricing or its client's.
- **B2. ## §4 Concerns left unproven (stated as such)

1. **The handwritten "30" marks on the exceptions page.** Someone hand-wrote "30" (or similar) and initials beside each of the three liftgate rows on 02-002 p. 22. 2. **NMFC claim rules under SEFL.** SEFL 1090 Item 100 makes NMF 100 a governing publication. NMFC's claim items (YRC's form quotes Item 300205; TForce 813 cites "NMFC series items 300500") may add procedure under SEFL. They are not held. As far as the files show, the 180-day window still comes from the statute, so the doc's "SEFL's tariff has none, so the statute governs" stands, unchallenged rather than proven.
3. **What FedEx 438 governs.** FedEx 438 bars "Any action or proceeding by Customer to recover overcharges" after 180 days from receipt plus 7 days. Whether a carrier would read this as a claim-filing deadline or only a suit deadline is a reading. Its ¶2-3 channel invoice disputes separately.
4. **Whether a tariff can shorten 13710(a)(3)(B).** Several tariffs start the clock earlier than "receipt of the bill" (ABF, AAA Cooper, Werner, ODFL, Central). Whether these bind a shipper against the statute is a legal question that no file answers. The doc's "compute every clock and show the earliest" is the safe design either way.
