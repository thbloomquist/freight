> **Gate:** "4-6 candidates on the shortlist, each with a savings-pool estimate and its assumptions written down." **Met** (§5, §7).
> **Founders' decisions (2026-09-15, §8):** the shortlist of five is confirmed; P-15 to P-17 are adopted (C16-C18); S4 runs in a new chat.
> **What this file does not do.** It does not do S4's full scoring, which scores fee feasibility (F1-F5), runs Cohen's and Gurley's checks and the sensitivity to each founder's weights, applies the strict durability test, and draws the plot. It does not do S5's interviews either. The scores here are desk scores on the 1 / 0.5 / 0 scale, as the S3b step asks.

## How to read this

**The two axes** (`0_criteria.md` §4; row rules in `2_knockouts.md` §5):

| Axis | What it asks | Rows (weights) |
|---|---|---|
| **A, is it worth winning?** | the prize | A1 savings pool (20); A2 fit for automation (10); A3 pain and urgency (25); A4 fragmentation (15); A5 frequency (15); A6 expansion leverage (15) |
| **W, can we win?** | our odds | W1 access (20); W2 team knowledge (5); W3 whole product and time to value (20); W4 competition and substitutes (15); W5 willingness to change (15); W6 time and resources to win (25) |

- **Scores:** each row is 1, 0.5 or 0, and each axis runs 0-100.
- **Per-side rows** (A3, A4, W1, W5) use the better of the card's two sides (C5).
- **W2 is 0 for every card** until domain immersion (F-15).

**How "top 4-6" is chosen [our call].** The S3b step says "keep the top 4-6", but not how two axes combine into one order.
- This file ranks cards by **the sum of the two axes (A + W, out of 200).** It keeps the cards above the first clear break inside 4-6, breaks ties on the central fee pool, and records how the order moves under other readings (§4).
- S4 keeps the axes separate and plots them. The sum is used here only to cut the list.
- The rule is proposed as a process change (P-15, §8.2).

**One reading applied to all 18 cards [our call]: A3 = 1 needs a dated rule, rule change or crisis that acts on the card's own job.**
- Triggers that act on the whole freight market (the 2026 rate rise, capacity) count 0.5.
- So do triggers that act on a neighbouring job, and rules still pending.
- On this reading, A3 = 1 for six cards:
  - C-08: *Montgomery* (May 2026), Motus (Q2 2026), the fraud crisis;
  - C-09: the NMFC dockets, which change LTL bills;
  - C-10: the cargo-theft record;
  - C-11: the NMFC dockets;
  - C-19: dated retailer rule changes;
  - C-20: de minimis ended, the tariff ruling, the refund process.
- This is proposed as P-16 (§8.2).

   - the saving needs physical or behavioural change;

4. **Being able to win is scarce.** The best W is 65. No card has data fully at hand, a single channel and weak incumbents at once. W2 (team knowledge) is 0 everywhere until domain immersion.
