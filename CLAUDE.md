## Constraints on what we look for
- **Software-improvable problems only.** Target processes that can be automated and currently cost too much. Physical or capital problems like trucks, fuel, and roads are out of scope unless software changes how they're used.
- **Problem first, solution second.** Pick the segment, find its PROBLEMS, and only then define a solution.
- **The fee has to come out of savings.** A problem only qualifies if it has a cost we can measure and our fee can be a share of it. *Clarified 2026-10-08 (C28): a subscription meets this rule. Founders: "We market it as a scoop off the top of the money they will make, but it will not be contingent on them actually making money."*
